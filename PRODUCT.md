# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CryptoARC is for a local operator running a Pump.fun / PumpPortal research, paper-trading, and gated live-execution workstation. The operator is usually watching source health, strategy confidence, PnL, wallet readiness, safety gates, and live-intent reviews while trying to decide whether the system is safe enough to keep running, tune, pause, or escalate into a capped live pilot.

## Product Purpose

CryptoARC exists to turn volatile launch-stream data into evidence-backed decisions before any real-money automation is trusted. Success means the dashboard makes source reliability, strategy confidence, risk state, execution readiness, and audit history obvious enough that the operator can move from local paper mode to tightly gated live execution without hidden assumptions or unsafe shortcuts.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, vigilant. The interface should feel like a serious local trading workstation: fast, legible, restrained, and alert to risk. Copy should be direct, second-person when action-oriented, and specific about blockers, next steps, and confidence.

## Anti-references

Do not make CryptoARC feel like a casino, meme-coin hype page, marketing landing page, neon toy terminal, generic AI-purple SaaS dashboard, or over-animated crypto game. Avoid decorative motion, mystery-meat controls, playful copy around money risk, low-contrast gray-on-gray data, and any UI pattern that hides safety gates behind visual spectacle.

## Design Principles

- Safety state before speed: show readiness, caps, kill switches, and blockers before encouraging execution.
- Evidence over vibes: every recommendation, signal, and automation step should point back to data quality, replay confidence, labels, source health, or audit records.
- Local-first trust: reinforce that signing, secrets, and live controls stay local and gated.
- Dense but scannable: support repeated operator use with compact panels, stable controls, clear hierarchy, and tabular numbers.
- Motion must explain state: animation should clarify loading, entry, selection, and feedback, never perform for its own sake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, keyboard access, focus visibility, labels, semantic controls, and error recovery. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Use color with text, icons, or labels rather than color alone for risk states. Keep numeric data readable with tabular figures and avoid layouts that require precision pointing for critical actions.
